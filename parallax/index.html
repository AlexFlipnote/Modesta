Parallax

A banner with a fixed, blurred background image and centred content on top. Set the background image on parallax yourself.

ClassDescription
parallax-containerWraps the background and the content
parallaxThe background, 20em tall
parallax.small15em tall
parallax.medium26em tall
parallax.large33em tall
contentContent centred over the background
