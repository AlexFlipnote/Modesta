Colours

Ready-made colours you can apply with a class, without writing any CSS. They also win over themed defaults, so label sun-flower-bg gives a yellow label.

ClassDescription
{colour}-textText in that colour
{colour}-bgBackground in that colour
{colour}-dropshadowBackground in that colour with a matching drop shadow
