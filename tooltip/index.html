Tooltip

Shows the data-tooltip text when hovering over an element. It appears below by default, add left or right to change that.

ClassDescription
tooltipShows the tooltip below the element
tooltip.leftShows it to the left
tooltip.rightShows it to the right

Source: scss/components/objects/_tooltip.scss
